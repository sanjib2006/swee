• 3 Requirements Errors: Anomaly (ambiguity), Inconsistency (contradiction), Incompleteness (omission).
• 4GL vs 3GL: 4GL is slower & uses 50% more RAM; rewriting in 3GL gives up to 10× speedup.
• Decision Trees vs Tables: Trees show execution sequence; Tables evaluate all condition combinations.
